## 4. Reviews

Reviews on Google Business Profile matter for Google Maps and for AI
assistants answering "best equipment rental in Sohar".

- Ask every customer after a job finishes, by WhatsApp, with the direct review link.
- Ask them to mention the machine and the area, e.g. "wheel loader for our Buraimi crusher".
- Reply to every review, good or bad, within a few days.
- Never buy reviews or write them yourself. Google removes them and can suspend the profile.

**WhatsApp message:**
> Hello [name], thank you for renting from Abdul Masood Trading for [job]. If you were happy with the service, a short Google review would really help us: [review link]. Mentioning the machine and the area helps other customers find us. Thank you!
